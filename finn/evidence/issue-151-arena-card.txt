ARENA PANE HEIGHT — the reviewer's clipping question, settled
--------------------------------------------------------------
The reviewer saw `CONCLUDED DUEL (v5)` clipped at the pane edge at
1726 x 1032 and could not tell whether that was their window geometry or the
card genuinely not being reachable.  Measured on the same geometry:

  - A scrollbar IS present in the ARENA pane, at x = 1398..1402.  Its handle
    spans y = 755..1031 in a track of y = 730..1064 — 276 px of 334, so the
    pane's content is about 1.2x its viewport, roughly 70 px taller.
  - The pane opens scrolled ~30 px down from its top, which is why the
    `ALPHA ARENA · BLIND FORECASTING CONTEXT` title is not visible in the
    deliverable shot.
  - It scrolls normally: one click on the scrollbar track above the handle
    (1400, 741) paged it to the top and brought the title into view — that
    is issue-151-arena-heading-clipped.png.  Nothing is unreachable.
  - Driven to BOTH extremes rather than derived from the handle arithmetic.
    At the bottom extreme (handle flush at 776..1031) the pane ends with
    `execution_eligible=false`, the record box's bottom border, and empty
    pane.  Nothing exists below the card.  The scroll range past the card is
    therefore phantom — word-wrapped QLabels over-reserving in sizeHint(),
    not content held back.

So at 1726 x 1032 the pane is ~30 px short of showing the section title and
the card's last line at the same time.  Either one is reachable by scrolling;
the CONCLUDED DUEL card itself fits whole in one frame, and there is nothing
below it at any scroll position.  Not filed as a defect — the reviewer
declined to, and it is outside issue #151's criteria.

One half of the reviewer's observation is NOT reproduced or refuted here: the
claim that wheel events over the pane propagate to the QTabBar and switch
tabs.  The automation available in this session can click and type but cannot
synthesise wheel events, so that was not tested.  Reported as untested rather
than as working.
