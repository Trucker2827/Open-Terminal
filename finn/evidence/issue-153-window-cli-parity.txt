The two are character-for-character the same because they are the same
function: `kalshi_bot_funnel_lines()` in KalshiBotFunnel.h. The panel unit test
asserts `view.funnel == kalshi_bot_funnel_lines(file, now)`; the e2e asserts the
CLI's human lines are the JSON's `funnel_lines` array. Neither surface derives
a number of its own.

Every line carries `· as of 3m ago` because the funnel file is older than two
tick intervals (kKalshiBotStaleMs) — criterion 4's staleness rule, rendering in
both places.

Known, NOT introduced here: the `â` in the FILL MODEL line is on disk.
KalshiBotOrders.cpp:291 writes a UTF-8 em-dash source literal through
QString::fromLatin1(), so every fill row since rung 6 carries the mojibake.
Criterion 4 requires printing the ledger's own value, so this renderer
faithfully reproduces it. Filed separately rather than fixed here.
