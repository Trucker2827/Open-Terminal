Issue #131 — Kalshi bot rung 6: order lifecycle honesty
=======================================================

Every transcript below is a real `openterminalcli kalshi bot once` run against a
SCRATCH evidence directory (OPENTERMINAL_KALSHI_EVIDENCE_DIR=/tmp/e131*) holding
a synthetic calibrator.json. The operator's live ledger was never written to.

Build: openmarketterminal-qt/build (ninja), full suite 130/130.


1. A bid opens a RESTING order, and the resting remainder is exposure
----------------------------------------------------------------------
mid 0.83, calibrated 0.95 → YES limit $0.83 × 2.

  KALSHI BOT · PAPER · ok
    bids 1 · passes 0 · open 0
    resting 1 ($1.66 at limit) · exposure $1.66 of $120.00 · session $1.68 of $120.00

`open 0` is the point: nothing has filled it, so it is not a position. The
$1.66 is 2 × the $0.83 LIMIT — the PR #44 rule.


2. The market moves past the quote → it stays resting, and is not re-quoted
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
mid 0.84 (above the $0.83 limit):

  bids 0 · passes 1 · open 0
  resting 1 ($1.66 at limit) · exposure $1.66 of $120.00

  ledger: pass QUOTE_RESTING     ← the bot replaces quotes, it never stacks them


3. The edge goes → the quote is cancelled
------------------------------------------
mid 0.90 against calibrated 0.95 (edge 0.05 < the 0.10 threshold):

  resting 0 ($0.00 at limit) · exposure $0.00 of $120.00
  filled 0 · canceled 1 · UNCONFIRMED cancels 0 (still counted as risk)

  ledger rows, in order:
    bid    EDGE_CLEARS_THRESHOLD  order_state=resting  2 @ 0.83
    pass   QUOTE_RESTING
    cancel CANCELED_EDGE_GONE     order_state=canceled 2 @ 0.83
    pass   EDGE_BELOW_THRESHOLD


4. The market comes back through the limit → it fills, at its own limit
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
mid 0.82 against a $0.83 limit:

  bids 0 · passes 1 · open 1
  resting 0 ($0.00 at limit) · exposure $1.66 of $120.00
  filled 1 · canceled 0 · UNCONFIRMED cancels 0

Filled at $0.83 (the limit), not at the better $0.82 the mid implies — the
improvement would be invented. The filled quantity is still exposure.


5. TTL expiry → cancel, then re-quote (cancel/replace)
-------------------------------------------------------
mid 0.845, so the floored limit ($0.84) rests BELOW the market and nothing
fills it. The order's ts_ms was then rewound 200s past its 180s TTL:

  bids 1 · passes 0 · open 0
  resting 1 ($1.68 at limit) · exposure $1.68 of $120.00
  filled 0 · canceled 1 · UNCONFIRMED cancels 0

  ledger rows, in order:
    bid    EDGE_CLEARS_THRESHOLD
    cancel CANCELED_TTL   resting_seconds=200.272
    bid    REQUOTED       replaces_position_id=KXBTC15M-E131-15@1784914959690

Note (1) and (5) together: with real calibrator mids (0.8342, not 0.83) the
floored limit rests strictly below the market, so a fill is genuinely
conditional on the market coming back — it is not a rounding artifact.

The second contract sees the first one's resting exposure inside the same

7. A resolved market cannot fill, and cannot be quoted again
-------------------------------------------------------------
The trap rung 1 closed with CONTRACT_SETTLED, re-opened by the lifecycle: the
report's snapshot goes stale under a fresh generation stamp, so a market that
has ALREADY resolved is still quoted — here at mid 0.82, through the $0.82
limit, i.e. everything about the report says "fill". A real
kalshi-settlements.jsonl row for the contract sat beside it.

  tick 1  bids 1 · resting 1 ($1.64 at limit)
  tick 2  filled 0 · canceled 1 · resting 0 · exposure $0.00
  tick 3  bids 0 · passes 1

  ledger:
    bid    EDGE_CLEARS_THRESHOLD
    cancel CANCELED_MARKET_SETTLED     ← not a fill
    pass   CONTRACT_SETTLED
    pass   CONTRACT_SETTLED

Both halves were found by review and each was seen failing first:
  settled checked AFTER the fill  → the tick 2 cancel becomes a FILL, and the
                                    "position" settles into fabricated P&L
  CANCELED_MARKET_SETTLED not fed
    back into the settled set     → tick 3 quotes a resolved contract again
                                    (the order left the book, and having never
                                    filled it produced no settlement row)

  neuters: each turns exactly
  a_market_that_settles_under_a_resting_quote_retires_it_without_a_position red.

  Also added after review: a_filled_positions_exposure_blocks_a_bid_on_another
  _contract — AC2 says "resting+filled exposure", and only the resting half was
